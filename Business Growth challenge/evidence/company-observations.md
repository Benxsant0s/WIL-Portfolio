# Company observations and fit analysis

What the two company sites showed on **30 October 2026**, and how each offering fits the Digital Readiness Check. Source: text extracted from [tobb.co.za](https://tobb.co.za/) (E01) and [coxygen.co/universities](https://coxygen.co/universities/index.php) (E02). Check the live sites, since they may have changed, and confirm the observations in section 3 before sharing them with anyone at Tobb.

## 1. Offering-to-pillar map

Every pillar of the Check connects to something Tobb or Coxygen already sells or teaches. This is the core of the "benefits the company as a whole" argument: the Check does not require inventing new services.

| Pillar | Tobb offering on the site (service code) | Coxygen offering | Supporting evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Online presence | Standard and dynamic websites (S5014, DW015), SEO (SE006), branded email (WE005), logo and graphic design (LD007, GD009) | none | E07, E03 |
| Operations and systems | CRM (CR31), ERP (ER32), project management (PM37), inventory and database apps (ID39), process flow and integration | none | E04, E06 |
| Payments and e-commerce | Simple online store (CS017), advanced commercial site (CA20), e-commerce app (ECW4) | none | E03, E05 |
| Data protection and security | Backups (BU008), cloud storage and solutions (CS003), hosting and support (WH002) | Cybersecurity and Digital Skills path | E08-E11 |

| Infrastructure and hosting | Domain registration and hosting (DR001, WH002), cloud, networks, computers, laptops, tablets | none | E06 |

## 2. Strengths the idea builds on

| Observation from the sites | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Operating since 2006, with portfolio systems for schools, municipalities, finance and business | Credibility behind the recommendations the Check makes |
| Tobb hosts and supports what it builds, and runs its own VPS | Can host the Check at low cost and keep data under its control |
| PHP/MySQL web stack and open-source tools | The Check, and any later lead database, fit the team's existing skills |
| Graduate internship programme; stated values of reducing unemployment and empowering women in IT | Readiness Clinics give interns real client work, and give the campaign a genuine social angle (E12, E14, E16, E17) |
| Coxygen has a training centre, events platform, certificates and community channels (Telegram, Discord, GitHub wiki) | Ready distribution channels and a destination for "Skills and people" results |
| Partner companies named on the Tobb site | Starting points for co-branded distribution and pilot users |

## 3. Observations that could reduce conversion (fix in Phase 0)

*From the extracted page text. Please verify on the live site.*

| Observation | Suggested fix |
|---|---|
| The WhatsApp number appears in two formats (one without the country code) | Use one format everywhere: +27 73 182 0631 |
| Several email domains (tobb.io addresses on a tobb.co.za site) | Choose a primary contact address and state it consistently |
| A link to COVID-19 information in the header | Remove or replace with a current resource |
| A "Free Hosting promotion" banner (a R3,580 saving) with no visible terms | Add terms and an end date, or remove |
| Several portfolio entries are placeholders (links to `#`) | Complete them with permission-based case studies, or hide them |
| No testimonials, results or pricing guidance in the extracted text | Add two or three client quotes and "from" prices for common packages |
| Typographical errors (for example "escrew", "easy your heavy load", "gallary") | Proofread |
| No visible link between Tobb and Coxygen services | Add a "Skills" section on Tobb and a "Need it built?" link on Coxygen, both pointing to the Check |

## 5. Do not claim

Avoid statements the evidence does not support: that the Check "guarantees" POPIA compliance; that Tobb is "the leading" provider of anything; any specific number of new clients before a pilot; or national-level conclusions from voluntary benchmark data.
